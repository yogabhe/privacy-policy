Privacy Policy for Zilan Print

Last updated: June 24, 2026

We built the Zilan Print app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by us at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data directly. The app functions entirely locally on your device. Any receipt or label templates, fonts, or print history you save are stored in your device's local database.

2. Device Permissions Required

  • Bluetooth: Used to discover and connect to nearby thermal or label printers.
  • Location: Required by the Android OS to perform Bluetooth scans. We do not track or save your location.
  • USB: Used to connect directly to USB printers.
