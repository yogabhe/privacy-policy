Privacy Policy for GZ-print

Last updated: October 12, 2026

We built the GZ-Print app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by us at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

The app works locally on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, store, or share your personal data ourselves. Any receipt or label templates, fonts, or print history you save are stored in your device's local database. Documents, photos, and PDF files you print are processed on your device and are not uploaded by us.

However, the app uses third-party services (see section 4) that may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system version, app usage events, and crash reports. This information is collected by those providers under their own privacy policies.

2. Device Permissions Required

  • Bluetooth: Used to discover and connect to nearby thermal or label printers.
  • Location: Required by the Android OS (Android 11 and lower only) to perform Bluetooth scans. We do not track or save your location.
  • USB: Used to connect directly to USB printers.
  • Internet: Used by the third-party services listed below (advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and app update checks). Printing itself does not require an internet connection.

3. Files and Photos

The app only accesses files and photos that you explicitly choose (using the system file or photo picker) or share to the app from another app. Text recognition (OCR) for receipts runs on your device and the content is not sent to us.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services (including in-app update and in-app review)
  • AdMob (advertising)
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (app usage statistics and crash reports)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent for personalized advertising)
  • Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition)

5. Advertising and Your Choices

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can review or change your choice at any time from the "About App" screen (Privacy Options) when available. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by the app on your device (templates, fonts, print history, settings) stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the app. Data collected by third-party services is governed by their own retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page together with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us
